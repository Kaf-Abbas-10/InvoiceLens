From document to details.
InvoiceLens uses geometry and explicit rules. It never sends your invoice to an LLM or external extraction service.
  1. Read locally. Digital PDFs use their text layer. Scans and images use a locally installed Tesseract engine.
  2. Follow the layout. Words form rows; aligned amounts identify charge columns. Each extracted line keeps its page and bounding box.
  3. Check the maths. Charge totals, tax, discounts, and job subtotals are reconciled with decimal arithmetic.
  4. Take a second look. Review flags explain missing fields, low OCR confidence, and mismatched totals. A passing check does not guarantee every field is correct.
Examples are synthetic layout fixtures, not a real-world accuracy benchmark. The confidence indicator is a rule heuristic, not a calibrated probability. Uploads are processed in memory, apart from temporary multipart spooling. Session history stays in browser memory and clears on refresh.
